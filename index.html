THE PROGRAM

A direct route to Brazilian sugar supply.

Project TITAN is designed for qualified international buyers seeking a direct commercial path to Brazilian sugar mills. Through the partnership with Ultra 7, qualified buyers may be connected to multiple mills, subject to availability, capacity and commercial approval.

This website presents only the current Brazil-based transaction model.

Core commercial structure

  • Direct connection to a network of 4+ mills.
  • Initial target volume up to 12,500 MT per mill.
  • Potential expansion up to 25,000 MT per mill, subject to approval.
  • Price negotiated according to crop, mill and market conditions.
  • Payment performed in Brazil under the agreed commercial contract.

TRANSACTION STRUCTURE

How the TITAN process works.

01

Buyer qualification

Company information, product, volume, destination and evidence of financial capacity.

02

Commercial alignment

Potenza / Ultra 7 align the request with available mills and current crop conditions.

03

Mill connection

The qualified buyer enters the commercial flow with the available mill or mills.

04

Contract & deposit

After contract execution, 30% is paid in advance to secure the contract and credited against the final shipment.

05

Load-and-pay

The remaining amount is paid as product is loaded — “carregou, pagou”.

06

Collection & logistics

The buyer must have the legal, financial and operational structure to collect and transport the product in Brazil.

BUYER REQUIREMENTS

Built for qualified buyers only.
