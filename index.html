THE NEW TITAN MODEL

One clear route to purchase.
No legacy sales programs.

Potenza TITAN focuses exclusively on this direct-purchase structure. Through the partnership with Ultra 7, qualified buyers may be introduced to participating Brazilian sugar mills and discuss available product, price and harvest terms directly with the mill.

Potenza TITAN coordinates the qualification and commercial communication process. The mill remains responsible for confirming product availability, final pricing, contract terms and acceptance of the transaction.

ImportantAll volumes, product specifications, prices and dates require written confirmation by the relevant mill. No supply is guaranteed by this website.

COMMERCIAL FRAMEWORK

Terms designed around
operational readiness.

The following are the program's proposed baseline conditions and remain subject to mill approval and a signed contract.

01

Volume per mill

Initial maximum of 12,500 metric tonnes per mill. Volumes up to 25,000 metric tonnes per mill may be considered following mill approval and availability.

02
R$

Payment in Brazil

Payments are made in Brazil using funds whose lawful availability and origin can be documented and verified through the agreed compliance process.

03
30%

Contract advance

A 30% advance is proposed to secure the contract and is credited against the final shipment, as detailed in the signed contract.

04
✓

Pay as loaded

The remaining amount is paid as each load is completed, before the loaded truck is released, under the mill's approved procedure.

05
⌁

Buyer pickup

The buyer must demonstrate sufficient legal and operational capacity to collect the product and arrange transport directly from the mill.

06
§

Legal verification

Foreign buyers must provide corporate, beneficial ownership, source-of-funds and Brazil-based funds evidence as reasonably required for compliance review.
