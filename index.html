THE PROGRAM

A focused route to Brazilian sugar supply.

Project TITAN is designed for qualified international buyers seeking a direct commercial path to Brazilian sugar mills. Through the Potenza Trading and Ultra 7 relationship, qualified buyers may be connected to one or more of the 4 participating mills, subject to availability, capacity and commercial approval.

The website presents only the current Brazil-based transaction model. Legacy structures are not part of this offer.

TRANSACTION STRUCTURE

Clear process. Qualified buyers. Direct execution.

01

Buyer Qualification

Corporate information, target product, volume, destination and proof of financial capacity.

02

Mill Matching

Potenza / Ultra 7 align the buyer request with one or more available Brazilian sugar mills.

03

Commercial Alignment

Price, crop conditions, availability and operational structure are aligned with the mill.

04

Contract & 30% Advance

After contract execution, 30% is paid in advance to secure the contract and credited against the final shipment.

05

Load & Pay

The remaining balance is paid as product is loaded — a “carregou, pagou” model.

06

Collection & Logistics

The buyer must maintain the legal and operational structure required to collect and transport product in Brazil.
